KEEP THE GAME FAIR

THE GROUND RULES.

How do rankings work?+

A win earns 3 points. Ties in points use score difference, then scores for. If exactly two players are still tied, their head-to-head result decides. Otherwise they share the rank. A final tied rank shares the crown. Players without results are unranked.

Can the same players meet twice?+

One official result per pair, whichever way the names are entered. Scores must be whole numbers from 0 to 6, with a clear winner. Capitalization, spaces and punctuation do not create a new identity. Different spellings found in screenshots must be mapped to the existing player.

How are screenshot results checked?+

Text recognition helps read the scoreboard. The uploader must check both players and final scores. The original image and any corrections remain visible. Anyone can flag a concern; open concerns hold the final crown. Screenshots and anonymous uploads cannot prove authenticity by themselves.

Can players join later?+

Add real participants before the first result. The roster then locks so the season has a fixed finish line. Match reminders are generated from the current results and can be copied and shared by you.

How do clips and reactions work?+

Post your own gameplay as MP4 or WebM, up to 20 MB. Each browser can choose one reaction per clip and change or remove it. Reactions never affect standings. Uploads and match evidence are public.
